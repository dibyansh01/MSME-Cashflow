# Insight Rules Engine

> **Status:** Specification (implemented in Phase 4)

## Core Insight Categories
1. **Broken Promise Spike:** Customer repeatedly commits and fails to pay within agreed horizon.
2. **Impending 43B(h) Clock:** Unpaid micro/small enterprise invoices nearing 15/45 day limit.
3. **ITC at Risk:** Vendor invoice nearing 180-day deadline without settlement.
4. **Credit Limit Breach:** Outstanding balance exceeds approved credit ceiling.
5. **Concentration Risk:** > 40% of total receivables tied to a single debtor.
